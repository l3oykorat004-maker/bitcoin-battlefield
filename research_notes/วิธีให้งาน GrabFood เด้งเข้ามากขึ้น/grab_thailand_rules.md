# Grab Thailand partner-performance rules for GrabFood bike riders (ไรเดอร์) — acceptance/cancellation, GrabBenefits tiers, เพชร/กะรัต incentives, commission, penalties (2025–2026)

**Research method note.** Direct fetching of grab.com, help.grab.com and grabdriverth.com was blocked by the session's egress proxy (403 on CONNECT) and WebFetch could not resolve those hosts, so every finding below comes from search-engine extracts of those pages (the search tool returned verbatim Thai passages from the page text). Page content is therefore quoted as surfaced by search, not read in full; where a page was found but its text was cut off, that is flagged as a gap. The per-turn web-search budget was exhausted before the last batch of follow-up queries could run; those unresolved items are listed under Gaps.

**Primary official sources identified.** Grab Thailand's partner site **GrabDriverTH (grabdriverth.com)** is the official Thai-language rulebook: it hosts "ระเบียบปฏิบัติ" (regulations, last updated 11 Aug 2026), "GrabAcademy" explainers, per-province incentive announcements and GrabBenefits tier pages. grab.com/th/understand-incentive/ is Grab's consumer-facing explainer of the incentive cycle. help.grab.com/driver/th-th could not be reached or surfaced in search.

## Key Question 1 — How does Grab Thailand define and compute อัตรารับงาน (acceptance rate) and อัตรายกเลิกงาน (cancellation rate) for delivery partners, over what period, and which actions count against the partner?

### Takeaway
Grab Thailand defines acceptance rate as jobs accepted ÷ jobs that appeared on the app ("เด้ง/ปรากฏ") ×100, and cancellation rate as jobs cancelled ÷ jobs accepted; a job you accept and then cancel does not hurt acceptance but hits cancellation. For account-health purposes the motorcycle standard (all areas, effective 19 May 2025) is evaluated every Monday on the previous 7 days (Mon–Sun); since 29 Oct 2025 a second, rolling 4-hour check of consecutive cancellations while online can trigger 1–15-minute temporary suspensions.

### Cited Findings
- Acceptance-rate formula (GrabDriverTH GrabAcademy): "จำนวนงานที่กดรับ หารด้วยจำนวนงานที่ปรากฏ แล้วคูณด้วย 100" — the system compares jobs the partner pressed accept on against all jobs that appeared on the app screen, shown as a percentage; the best possible is 100% — [GrabDriverTH: อัตราการรับงาน](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grabacademy/arcr); same formula restated on [GrabDriverTH: [GrabBike] คะแนนต่างๆบนหน้าแอปพลิเคชันคืออะไร](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/ar-cr-rating)
- Cancellation-rate formula: the system compares the number of jobs cancelled against the number of jobs accepted; best is 0%. Worked examples on the official page: accept 2 jobs, cancel 1 → 50%; "กดรับงาน 4 งาน ยกเลิก 1 งาน คะแนนการยกเลิกงานจะเท่ากับ 25 เปอร์เซ็นต์" — [GrabDriverTH: อัตราการรับงาน](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grabacademy/arcr)
- Interaction rule stated by Grab: "หากกดรับงานแล้วกดยกเลิก คะแนนการรับงานจะไม่ลดลง แต่จะเสียคะแนนการยกเลิกงานแทน" (accept-then-cancel does not lower acceptance score, it lowers cancellation score) — [GrabDriverTH: อัตราการรับงาน](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grabacademy/arcr)
- Implication of the formula: a job that appears and is neither accepted (ignored/timed-out or declined) counts in the denominator of acceptance rate; only pressed-accept jobs count in the numerator — [GrabDriverTH: อัตราการรับงาน](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grabacademy/arcr)
- Measurement window for the motorcycle quality standard: "ระบบจะประเมินอัตราการรับ-ยกเลิกทุกวันจันทร์ โดยคำนวณจากอัตราการรับ / ยกเลิก ใน 7 วันย้อนหลัง" (evaluated every Monday from the previous 7 days, Mon–Sun); counters were reset and recomputed from new data only from 19 May 2025 (19 พ.ค. 68) — [GrabDriverTH: [รถจักรยานยนต์] มาตรฐานคุณภาพการให้บริการ](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grabacademy/2w-cr)
- The motorcycle standard page itself states it originally took effect 28 Nov 2022 ("มีผลบังคับใช้ตั้งแต่วันที่ 28 พฤศจิกายน 2565 เป็นต้นไป"), with the May 2025 recalculation layered on top — [GrabDriverTH: [รถจักรยานยนต์] มาตรฐานคุณภาพการให้บริการ](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grabacademy/2w-cr)
- Newer cancellation regulation ("ระเบียบปฏิบัติว่าด้วยเรื่องการยกเลิกงาน") for all motorcycle service types, effective 29 Oct 2568 (2025): the system counts consecutive cancellations and the cancellation rate every 4 hours while the partner is online; unusually high consecutive cancellations result in temporary service suspension of 1 to 15 minutes; the page also mentions an "8-second cancellation" feature (text cut off in search extract) — [GrabDriverTH: ระเบียบปฏิบัติว่าด้วยเรื่องการยกเลิกงาน](https://www.grabdriverth.com/cancellation-rate-standard); announced for Bangkok bikes at [GrabDriverTH: [กรุงเทพฯ🛵] ร่วมยกระดับมาตรฐานการให้บริการ](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2026/bike/cost-to-reject-timeout-bangkok)
- A parallel provincial (ต่างจังหวัด) page sets conditions for a temporary "ไม่สามารถรับรายการได้ชั่วคราว" (temporarily unable to receive orders) status "หากยกเลิกหรือเปิดให้บริการโดยไม่เจตนา" (if cancelling, or going online unintentionally) — [GrabDriverTH: [ต่างจังหวัด 🛵] เงื่อนไข "ไม่สามารถรับรายการได้ชั่วคราว"](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2026/bike/cost-to-reject-timeout-otc); listed in the regulations hub updated 11 Aug 2026 — [GrabDriverTH: ระเบียบปฏิบัติ](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grab-regulation)
- Exempt cancellations: Grab states that certain cancellations are not counted toward the cancellation score if they meet the conditions and do not exceed a set number of times ("แกร็บได้ยกเว้นกรณียกเลิกงานที่จะไม่ส่งผลต่อคะแนนยกเลิกงาน (ไม่เกินจำนวนครั้งที่กำหนด)") — [GrabDriverTH: อัตราการรับงาน](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grabacademy/arcr); a GrabFood-specific page "เงื่อนไขการยกเลิกงาน เมื่อเกิดเหตุจำเป็น" exists but its text was not surfaced — [GrabDriverTH: gf-modified-cr](https://www.grabdriverth.com/gf-bkk/gf-modified-cr)
- Passenger no-show exemption (transport services): a partner may cancel without affecting the cancellation rate after waiting at least 10 minutes at the pickup point after pressing "arrived" (20 minutes for GrabDriveYourCar) — [GrabDriverTH: หาผู้โดยสารไม่พบ ยกเลิกรายการได้ไม่เสียคะแนน](https://www.grabdriverth.com/newfeatures/pax-no-show)
- Star rating (คะแนนดาว) definition: computed automatically from all customers who rated, over the last 100 trips ("ย้อนหลัง 100 เที่ยวล่าสุด"); customers who did not rate are excluded — [GrabDriverTH: คะแนนดาว](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grabacademy/star-rating); [GrabDriverTH: มาตรฐานการให้บริการ หรือ คะแนนดาว](https://www.grabdriverth.com/qc-star-rating)
- Car (4-wheel) standard, for comparison: from 26 Apr 2021 acceptance ≥80% and cancellation ≤10%, 7-day look-back, in-app notification when failing — [GrabDriverTH: [รถยนต์] มาตรฐานคุณภาพการบริการ](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/4w-ar-cr)
- Secondary/unverified (Pantip, Chiang Mai driver's account): in manual-accept mode the app gives roughly 15 seconds to decide; drivers who decline too often are forced into a minimum-acceptance mode or paused ~15–30 minutes — [Pantip 42350347](https://pantip.com/topic/42350347) (rider testimony, not official)
- Non-Thai comparison (Philippines, 2018, historical): Grab there computes a "completion rate" from completed bookings, cancellations and ignored jobs; excessive ignoring/cancelling triggers temporary lockouts — [Grab PH blog: Update on Cancellation Policy](https://www.grab.com/ph/blog/update-on-cancellation-policy/)

### Inferences
- Because the acceptance denominator is "jobs that appeared", letting a ping time out and pressing decline are treated identically for acceptance rate; the only way to protect acceptance is to accept. Cancelling after acceptance protects acceptance rate at the cost of cancellation rate — which explains why the Korat rider's report can show 81.8% acceptance and 6.3–8.8% cancellation simultaneously.
- Two different clocks now run in parallel for bikes: the weekly Monday evaluation (7-day window) governs warnings/suspension ladders, while the 4-hour rolling check (since 29 Oct 2025) governs short 1–15-minute lockouts for streaks of cancellations.
- The "อัตราสำเร็จ" (completion/success rate) label sometimes seen in weekly reports is not defined on any Thai page surfaced; the Philippine "completion rate" concept (completed ÷ (completed + cancelled + ignored)) is the closest published Grab definition but is not confirmed for Thailand.

### Gaps
- The full text of the 29 Oct 2025 cancellation regulation (what the "8-second" rule is — likely a grace window to cancel without penalty — and what counts as "unusually high consecutive cancellations") was cut off in search extracts; the page could not be fetched.
- Whether merchant-initiated or customer-initiated cancellations are excluded from the rider's cancellation rate for GrabFood is on the gf-modified-cr page, whose text was not surfaced.
- Exact decision timer for a ping (official figure) and whether "ignore" vs "decline" are logged differently anywhere were not found on official pages.
- Note on dates: the Bangkok announcement sits under a /news/2026/ URL while the extract states 29 Oct 2568 (2025); it is possible the effective date is actually 29 Oct 2569 (2026, i.e. four days after this note). Treat the year as unconfirmed.

## Key Question 2 — What thresholds does Grab Thailand publish (e.g. cancel ≤5%, acceptance ≥90%) for incentives, tiers and account health?

### Takeaway
Three different threshold sets exist and are often conflated: (a) account-health for motorcycles since 19 May 2025 is lenient — acceptance >50% and cancellation ≤10% weekly — with a warning/suspension ladder; (b) incentive campaigns impose their own, stricter gates, and official 2025 campaign texts use exactly the 90%/85% acceptance and 5% cancellation figures seen in the rider's reports; (c) GrabBenefits tier promotion requires a 4.7+ star rating plus unspecified acceptance/cancellation levels and a points target.

### Cited Findings
- Account-health (bikes, all areas, from 19 May 2025): "มาตรฐานอัตราการรับ-ยกเลิกรายการสำหรับผู้ให้บริการรถจักรยานยนต์ทุกพื้นที่ เริ่ม 19 พ.ค. 68" — acceptance more than 50% and cancellation not more than 10% — [GrabDriverTH: [รถจักรยานยนต์] มาตรฐานคุณภาพการให้บริการ](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grabacademy/2w-cr)
- Consequence ladder (same page): 1st–2nd failure → warning via the in-app message box ("ครั้งที่ 1-2 ตักเตือน ผ่านทางกล่องข้อความ ในแอปฯ คนขับ"); under the update effective 7 Sep 2026, the 8th offence brings a 14-day temporary signal suspension (ระงับสัญญาณชั่วคราว), the 9th a 30-day suspension, and the 10th a suspension notice by SMS that can be appealed — [GrabDriverTH: [รถจักรยานยนต์] มาตรฐานคุณภาพการให้บริการ](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grabacademy/2w-cr) (as extracted in a search on the appeal programme, [GrabDriverTH: Grab a Chance](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grabachance))
- Minimum star rating for account standing: GrabBike, GrabExpress and GrabFood partners must hold 4.50 stars or higher — [GrabDriverTH: มาตรฐานการให้บริการ หรือ คะแนนดาว](https://www.grabdriverth.com/qc-star-rating)
- Campaign thresholds matching the rider's report — Songkran 2025 Bangkok bike campaign: "อัตราการรับตลอดวันที่ 12 - 16 เม.ย. 68 มากกว่า 90%; อัตราการยกเลิกตลอดวันที่ 12 - 16 เม.ย. 68 น้อยกว่า 5%" for the main prize; the daily trip-accumulation reward used "อัตราการรับมากกว่า 85% และอัตราการยกเลิกไม่เกิน 5%" — [GrabDriverTH: [สงกรานต์ ส่งแกร็บ🛵] ฉ่ำสุขทั่วไทย](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2025/bike/songkrangrab-bangkok)
- Tier (GrabBenefits) gate: partners must meet all conditions — "จำนวนแต้มตามที่กำหนด, อัตราการรับและอัตราการยกเลิกตามที่กำหนด" and a cumulative rating of 4.7 stars or higher, plus uniform/equipment compliance — [GrabDriverTH: [พัทยา 🚗] เข้าร่วมเป็นเทพ - เซียน](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2025/car/tiering-pattaya); provincial bike version effective May 2025 lists the same categories — [GrabDriverTH: [ต่างจังหวัด🛵] เข้าร่วมเป็นเทพ - เซียน (batch2-g4)](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2025/bike/tiering-batch2-g4)
- Historical (2019) incentive gate for Bangkok taxis: acceptance >50% and cancellation <15% — [GrabDriverTH: ประกาศเงื่อนไขการรับอินเซนทีฟ](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/new-incentive-conditions) (old, retained only as history)
- Non-Thai comparison: Malaysia GrabBenefits Gold/Platinum require cancellation ≤4% and rating ≥4.9 with 50/350/1,000 quarterly rides — [Grab MY: GrabBenefits](https://www.grab.com/my/grab-benefits/); Philippines 2018: 5% cancellation for incentives, ≥10% weekly → sanctions — [Inquirer](https://business.inquirer.net/?p=249694)
- Grab's generic statement that incentive conditions differ every week and must be checked in the "อินเซนทีฟ" menu of the Grab Driver app — [Grab TH: understand-incentive](https://www.grab.com/th/understand-incentive/)

### Inferences
- The rider's two failed schemes (cancellation 6.3–8.8% vs a ≤5% gate; acceptance 81.8% vs a ≥90% gate) are incentive-eligibility failures, not account-health failures: at those numbers the rider is comfortably inside the 50%/10% account standard and should not be receiving warnings on that basis.
- Because incentive thresholds are campaign-specific, the 90%/5% pair should be read from the week's incentive card rather than assumed constant; Grab's own 2025 campaign used both 90% and 85% acceptance gates in the same promotion.

### Gaps
- Steps 3–7 of the motorcycle penalty ladder (what happens between the second warning and the 8th-offence 14-day suspension — e.g., short lockouts or reduced job allocation) were not visible in extracts.
- The exact acceptance/cancellation percentages required for each GrabBenefits tier ("ตามที่กำหนด") are not stated in the extracts; they may be in the in-app GrabBenefits page.
- No official text was found stating that Grab Thailand reduces job dispatch ("ลดงาน") for low metrics short of a suspension.

## Key Question 3 — How does the เพชร/กะรัต (diamond/karat) system work in 2025–2026, what are the tiers, and does any tier give job priority or visibility?

### Takeaway
"อินเซนทีฟสะสมเพชร" is a weekly accumulation incentive in which each completed job earns a karat value that varies by service type (e.g., GrabFood = 4 กะรัต in Grab's own example), by zone, by time and — since the 2025 tier rollout — by rider level; the Thai tier names are พลทหาร → จอมยุทธ์ → เซียน → เทพ, recalculated monthly and refreshed on the 4th of the following month. Official pages tie tiers to higher diamond incentives, cash-back, insurance and booking/Turbo quotas, but no surfaced page states that higher tiers receive dispatch priority.

### Cited Findings
- Definition: "อินเซนทีฟแบบสะสมเพชร เป็นรูปแบบที่พาร์ทเนอร์สามารถรวมผลงานไว้ในรูปแบบของ 'เพชร'" — each job type carries a different karat value, e.g., GrabFood earns 4 กะรัต, and values may change week to week; partners can earn extra diamonds by completing special missions/conditions shown in the "อินเซนทีฟ" menu — [GrabDriverTH GrabAcademy: อินเซนทีฟ](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grabacademy/incentive)
- Weekly cycle: counted per 7-day round starting Monday 00:00 and cut off Sunday 23:59 — [Grab TH: understand-incentive](https://www.grab.com/th/understand-incentive/)
- Newer bike diamond incentive (announcement ≈ mid-2025): "อินฯ สะสมเพชร ใหม่! สะสมง่าย ได้มากกว่าเดิม" — easier accumulation, more diamonds for large orders, restricted to partners using the order-receiving device (อุปกรณ์รับออเดอร์) — [GrabDriverTH: 2w-inc-merge-new23](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2w-inc-merge-new23)
- Diamond zones/time windows: a redesigned in-app page shows where and when diamonds can be collected — [GrabDriverTH: รู้ทันทุกพื้นที่เก็บเพชร](https://www.grabdriverth.com/newfeatures/area-time-gems); GrabFood adjusted per-job bonus rates and diamond-collection areas according to demand area — [GrabDriverTH: แกร็บฟู้ดปรับอัตราโบนัสต่องาน และพื้นที่สะสมเพชร](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/gf-new-ppr-area)
- Historical mechanics (2020 Bangkok "ภารกิจนักล่าเพชร"): diamonds per job depended on partner level, job type and zone; bonus rose with diamonds accumulated per day; example conversion 1 THB per karat rising to 2.9 THB per karat at higher counts ("ยิ่งเพชรเยอะยิ่งได้รับโบนัสเยอะ") — [GrabDriverTH: ภารกิจนักล่าเพชร](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/gem-hunter) (historical)
- Historical "ขุมทรัพย์" treasure zones: special high-demand zones; partner must reach within a time limit; cancelling a job in the zone forfeits the extra diamonds — [GrabDriverTH: ภารกิจพิชิตขุมทรัพย์](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/treasurebox) (historical)
- Tier names (bike, Phuket page): เทพ, เซียน, จอมยุทธ์, พลทหาร — [GrabDriverTH: [ภูเก็ต 🛵] เข้าร่วมเป็นเทพ - เซียน](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2025/bike/tiering-phuket); teaser branding "ท่องยุทธภพ แกร็บสิทธิประโยชน์" — [GrabDriverTH: tiering-teaser](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2025/tiering-teaser)
- Points: "จำนวนรอบขับของผู้ให้บริการจะถูกเปลี่ยนเป็น 'จำนวนแต้ม'" — trips are converted to points, points per trip differ by service type and time period, and points are used only for tier promotion — [GrabDriverTH: [ต่างจังหวัด 🚗] tiering-batch2-g5](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2025/car/tiering-batch2-g5)
- Tier period: criteria counted over the calendar month; "จะอัปเดตระดับผู้ขับขี่ใหม่ทุกวันที่ 4 ของเดือนถัดไป" (tier refreshed on the 4th of the following month) — [GrabDriverTH: [ภูเก็ต 🚗] tiering-phuket](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2025/car/tiering-phuket); monthly recalculation from prior-month points/acceptance/cancellation — [GrabDriverTH: [พัทยา 🛵] tiering-pattaya](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2025/bike/tiering-pattaya)
- Earlier tier-condition change for provincial bikes: new point conditions effective 1 Jul 2024 for the 1 Aug 2024 tier update — [GrabDriverTH: [ต่างจังหวัด🛵] เงื่อนไขใหม่! เพื่อเลื่อนระดับ (2w-otc5)](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grab-benefits/2w-otc5-new-tier); sibling page [2w-otc3-new-tier](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grab-benefits/2w-otc3-new-tier)
- Tier-linked diamond incentive: Bangkok bikes have a weekly incentive titled "อินเซนทีฟสะสมเพชร ยิ่งระดับสูง ยิ่งได้เยอะ" (higher tier, more earned) in the incentive-card menu; a cash-back incentive whose value depends on rider level, grouped by the last 3 months' tier; the page states Grab pays that incentive on the Wednesday of the 2nd week of the following month — [GrabDriverTH: [กรุงเทพฯ 🛵] เข้าร่วมเป็นเทพ - เซียน](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2025/bike/tiering-bangkok)
- Provincial bike incentive roundups state the "อินสะสมเพชร" shown is for ผู้ขับขี่ระดับเทพ only, with other levels told to check the incentive menu — [GrabDriverTH: [พัทยา 🏍️] รวมให้แล้ว! อินสำหรับทุกประเภทบริการสองล้อ](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2024/bike/pyxincentive); the Nakhon Ratchasima roundup offered diamond incentive up to 445 THB/day and continuous-booked-trip incentive up to 525 THB/day, valid only until 30 Nov 2025 — [GrabDriverTH: [นครราชสีมา 🏍️] รวมให้แล้ว!](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2024/bike/nakincentive)
- Tier benefits beyond incentives: motorcycle insurance for ระดับเทพ riders — [GrabDriverTH: [รถจักรยานยนต์] ประกันรถจักรยานยนต์สำหรับผู้ขับขี่ระดับเทพ](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2025/bike/god-insurance); GrabBenefits overview says benefits are granted by level and cover accident, vehicle, health and income protection — [GrabDriverTH: GrabBenefits สิทธิประโยชน์คนขับ](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grabacademy/driver-benefits); "ประกันอุบัติเหตุอุ่นใจ" covers during service and up to 30 minutes after going offline, "อุ่นใจพลัส" covers 24 hours — same page cluster (announcement ≈ Aug 2026)

- March 2026 GrabBenefits partnership with HDmall: 7% discount on health check-ups and women's cancer screening for all driver types — [HDmall press release](https://hdmall.co.th/blog/press-releases/grab-driver-thailand-hdmall-health-checkup-benefits-for-drivers/)

### Inferences

## Key Question 4 — What does Grab Thailand officially say about how to get more jobs (location, online time, batches, equipment, settings such as auto-accept, multi-service toggles, shift booking)?

### Takeaway
Grab Thailand's official levers are: keep acceptance high and cancellations low (they gate both incentives and tiers), book shifts/zones ("จองรอบขับ", Turbo/บัลลังก์ทอง programmes), work the published peak windows (10:30–13:30 and 16:00–20:00 for food/mart/express bonuses; 18:00–22:00 is Grab's stated busiest period), use the order-receiving device for the richer diamond incentive, keep enough job credit so jobs are not hidden, and configure service types and "รับรายการอัตโนมัติ" in Profile > All settings.

### Cited Findings
- Job credit: Grab's driver FAQ says a service fee is deducted from the partner's job-credit balance each time a job is accepted, and partners with too little credit do not see certain jobs — [Grab TH: driver bike FAQ](https://www.grab.com/th/en/driver/bike/) (same FAQ on [grab.com/th/driver/food](https://www.grab.com/th/driver/food/))
- Auto-accept: the lightning icon ⚡ at top-right shows status — green means "รับรายการอัตโนมัติ" is on, grey means off; pressing the icon does not toggle it — go to "โปรไฟล์ของฉัน" > "การตั้งค่าทั้งหมด" to turn it on/off — [GrabDriverTH: รับงานอัตโนมัติ](https://www.grabdriverth.com/auto-accept); the quick menu is a shortcut to auto-accept, "จุดหมายปลายทางของฉัน" and service-type settings — [GrabDriverTH: GrabVan landing](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grabvan-landing)
- Service-type toggles: partners can switch on GrabExpress multi-stop themselves in the app — [GrabDriverTH: GrabExpress Multi Stop](https://www.grabdriverth.com/service/ge-multistop); enabling GrabFood (Walk) blocks other job types ("พาร์ทเนอร์ที่เปิดรับงานบริการนี้ จะไม่สามารถรับงานประภทอื่นได้") — [GrabDriverTH: GrabFood (Walk)](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grabfood-walk)
- Shift booking: "จองรอบขับ" hub for two-wheel partners — [GrabDriverTH: จองรอบขับ](https://www.grabdriverth.com/delivery-home); Bangkok bike FAQ distinguishes a booking incentive (complete any booked slot) from an accumulation incentive (complete trips of the specified service type within the slot) — [GrabDriverTH: [กรุงเทพฯ🛵] ตารางรายการจองและการจองรอบ](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2025/bike/one-grab-shift-bangkok); bike "โปรแกรมรอบขับเทอร์โบ บัลลังก์ทอง" — book the Golden-Throne zone in the area/time you want to earn the Turbo incentive — [GrabDriverTH: pre-signup-streak](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2025/bike/pre-signup-streak); Turbo programme overview — [GrabDriverTH: turbo-home](https://www.grabdriverth.com/turbo-home)
- Priority booked slots: "รอบจองลำดับพิเศษ" incentives pay up to roughly +375–700 THB/day depending on city and dates — [GrabDriverTH: [หัวหิน] incentives 2026](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2026/bike/hua-hin-incentives)
- Peak windows: "มื้อเร่งด่วน" per-trip bonus (+฿3/trip Hat Yai; +฿5/trip Chiang Mai 2024) counts only GrabFood, GrabMart and GrabExpress trips in 10:30–13:30 and 16:00–20:00 — [GrabDriverTH: [หาดใหญ่] incentives](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2024/bike/hdyincentive); [GrabDriverTH: [เชียงใหม่ 🏍️] incentives](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2024/bike/cnxincentive)

### Inferences
- The clearest official "get more jobs" mechanisms are the booking/Turbo systems (guaranteed slot incentives) and tier progression (higher quotas and bigger diamond rungs) rather than any disclosed dispatch-priority rule.
- An 81.8% acceptance rate is the single metric most likely costing the Korat rider money: it fails the ≥90% incentive gate while being irrelevant to account health; auto-accept would push it toward 100% at the cost of control over job choice.

### Gaps
- No official Thai page titled "เคล็ดลับรับงานมากขึ้น" was surfaced; advice above is assembled from rule and campaign pages.
- Whether "รับงานหลายบริการ" (multi-service acceptance) or enabling GrabMart/GrabExpress alongside GrabFood increases dispatch volume is not stated officially.
- The current Korat shift-booking table (slots, quotas by tier) was not surfaced.

## Key Question 5 — Commission/fee structure for GrabFood vs GrabExpress (bike) and what "การปรับยอด" (adjustment/top-up) is

### Takeaway
Since the "EBP" restructure (Bangkok 3 Apr 2023; provinces 15 May 2024) GrabFood/GrabMart per-trip pay already has the commission built in, so riders see no deduction on food orders, whereas GrabExpress parcels still show an explicit commission (official OTC page: 25% on a 100-THB base; Shop 20%; TikTok Shop 15%). "ปรับยอด/ปรับรายได้" appears in Grab's new net-earnings display as income adjustments — cash-job reconciliation and Grab's top-up of the difference when a fare or guarantee is below the floor.

### Cited Findings
- GrabFood/GrabMart restructure ("GrabFood / GrabMart ปรับโครงสร้างค่ารอบใหม่"): provincial pages say from 15 May 2024 the commission is included in the per-trip fee rather than deducted; the Bangkok page gives 3 Apr 2023 and says GFC/GFH providers no longer have commission deducted; Grab sets a minimum fee per job and food wait time is built into the fee (no separate wait claims) — [GrabDriverTH: ebp/bangkok](https://www.grabdriverth.com/ebp/bangkok); [ebp/nakhon-pathom](https://www.grabdriverth.com/ebp/nakhon-pathom); [ebp/loe](https://www.grabdriverth.com/ebp/loe); [ebp/nang-rong](https://www.grabdriverth.com/ebp/nang-rong)
- GrabExpress (Bike) provincial page: base fare 100 THB, commission 25% ("ค่าบริการเริ่มต้น 100 บาท คอมมิชชั่น 25%"), 8 THB/km for first 10 km then 9 THB/km, extra drop-off 30 THB per stop from the 2nd stop — [GrabDriverTH: บริการรับส่งพัสดุ-เอกสาร (GrabExpress)](https://www.grabdriverth.com/service/ge-otc) (page ≈ early 2025)
- GrabExpress (Shop) Bangkok: base 50 THB, commission 20% — [GrabDriverTH: GrabExpress (Shop)](https://www.grabdriverth.com/service/ge-shop); GrabExpress (TikTok Shop): commission 15%, starting round rate 36 THB — [GrabDriverTH: GrabExpress TikTok Shop](https://www.grabdriverth.com/service/grabexpress-tiktok-shop)
- GrabExpress (Bike) 2022 per-km round rates: 7.4 THB/km (0–12 km), 7.6 (12.01–20), 8.1 (20.01–30), 14 (30.01+) — [GrabDriverTH: ge-fare-updates 2022](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2022/ge-fare-updates) (historical)
- Fee mechanics: Grab deducts the service fee from the job-credit wallet when a job is accepted — [Grab TH: driver bike FAQ](https://www.grab.com/th/en/driver/bike/); extra charges collected from customers are deducted from the driver's credit wallet (4-wheel breakdown) — [GrabDriverTH: 4w-fare-break-down](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2023/4w-fare-break-down)

- Top-up mechanics (cars, Bangkok, from 10 Mar 2025): "แกร็บสมทบส่วนต่าง เพื่อปรับรายได้ให้เพิ่มขึ้น" — Grab contributes the difference when the passenger pays less than the actual distance/time warrants (excl. GrabTaxi, Rent) — [GrabDriverTH: [กรุงเทพฯ🚗] โครงสร้างแบบใหม่ รับรายได้ตามจริง](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/eagle-bkk)
- Guarantee mechanics (bikes, older): per-job minimum income guarantee by time slot (morning/midday/evening/weekend); if the fare is below the guarantee, Grab pays the difference; an "average minimum guarantee" variant also exists — [GrabDriverTH: [GrabBike] อินเซนทีฟใหม่](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/gb-incentive) (old); hourly-average guarantee explainer — [GrabDriverTH: การันตีเฉลี่ยต่อชั่วโมง](https://www.grabdriverth.com/hourly-guarantee-info)
- Wallets: cash wallet and credit wallet; if credit is short the system auto-moves cash to credit; withdraw once balance ≥100 THB to the registered bank account, no fee — [GrabDriverTH: กระเป๋าเงินคนขับ](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grabacademy/driverwallet); [GrabDriverTH: bay-cashout](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grabacademy/bay-cashout)

- The rider's weekly report showing 20% commission only on GrabExpress parcels is consistent with the EBP rule that food/mart pay is net of commission; the 20% (vs the 25% on the provincial OTC page) may reflect a Korat-specific or later rate, or a Shop-type parcel.
