# Thai Grab rider community tactics for making more GrabFood jobs "เด้ง" — claims, spread, evidence, risks

*Evidence basis and limits (read first).* All material below comes from search-result summaries and snippets of Thai pages (Pantip, GrabDriverTH = Grab Thailand's own driver-partner communication site, Thai news, TikTok/Lemon8 captions, Grab regional policy pages). Full-page fetches were impossible in this environment (egress policy blocked every host; pantip.com, grabdriverth.com, prachatai.com, lemon8 etc. all unreachable), and the session's web-search budget ran out before every planned follow-up. Consequently: (1) Thai quotes are verbatim only where the search tool surfaced them; (2) comment threads on Pantip could not be read in full, so "how widely repeated" is judged from the number of independent threads/posts surfacing the same claim, not from comment counts; (3) Facebook rider groups, Blockdit and Dek-D were not reachable at all. Evidence grades used throughout: **[OFFICIAL]** = Grab statement/page; **[CONSENSUS]** = same claim in 3+ independent rider sources; **[ANECDOTE]** = 1–2 rider sources; **[UNSUPPORTED]** = repeated belief with no evidence found and/or contradicted by official material; **[TOS/ILLEGAL]** = violates Grab terms or Thai law — never a recommendation.

## Key Question 1 — What do riders report as the top reasons jobs stop coming (งานไม่เข้า / งานเงียบ), and which fixes reportedly worked?

### Takeaway
Thai riders overwhelmingly describe "งานไม่เข้า" as opaque and attribute it to five things: (a) the booking-slot/zone system (จองรอบ/โซน) concentrating jobs on riders with a regular zone and booked slots; (b) oversupply of riders vs. orders, especially upcountry; (c) acceptance/cancellation behaviour feeding back into allocation; (d) phone/notification problems; and (e) a vaguely described "ระบบเลือกคน". No fix is documented as reliably working; the only official levers found are booking slots (รอบจอง / รอบจองลำดับพิเศษ), Auto Accept, and keeping AR/CR inside incentive gates.

### Cited Findings
- **Opaque allocation is the dominant complaint [CONSENSUS].** A rider "อยู่แทบจะติดร้านค้าแล้ว แต่ระบบเหมือนเลือกจ่ายงานให้บางคนเท่านั้น"; another: "คนไหนที่ได้ก็ได้อยู่แบบนั้น" while he got nothing for hours — [Pantip 42421283 งานไม่ค่อยจ่ายเหมือนคนอื่น](https://pantip.com/topic/42421283); a rider asks why the system repeatedly pays certain riders even when he is near the merchant: "ผมอยากรู้ทำไมง่ายไม่จ่ายร้านค้าแนะนำเราเลย" — [Pantip 41575171 ไม่เข้าใจระบบ การจ่ายงาน Grab](https://pantip.com/topic/41575171); a 2025/26 thread reports ~3 weeks of far fewer jobs than others in the same area after a previously normal share, with no resolution surfaced — [Pantip 44085420 Grab ไม่จ่ายงานให้ แต่คนอื่นๆ ได้รับงานปกติ](https://pantip.com/topic/44085420); another asks why he got 2 jobs from morning to noon while others in the same area got ~20 — [Pantip search summary](https://pantip.com/topic/42421283).
- **Returning after a break → silence [ANECDOTE].** "ขับGRABมาได้ 1อาทิตย์แล้ว ปิดแอปหยุดไปทำธุระไม่ได้วิ่งงาน2วัน พอมาเปิดจะวิ่งงานรอทั้งวันไม่มีงานเด้งเข้าเลย" — [Pantip 41143613](https://pantip.com/topic/41143613). New rider with all documents approved got zero orders in first two days — [Pantip 41332519](https://pantip.com/topic/41332519).
- **Support gives stock answers [ANECDOTE].** A GrabFood Walk rider says support repeats "ระบบทำงานปกติ", account has no issue, reinstall the app and log in again — [Pantip 39976139](https://pantip.com/topic/39976139).
- **Booking-slot/zone system blamed for uneven distribution [CONSENSUS, news-documented].** In Nov 2022, 500+ Grab riders protested that "ต้องจองวัน เวลา และโซนที่จะรับงาน ส่งผลให้การกระจายงานไม่ทั่วถึง"; one group said daily orders fell from 20–30 to under 10; the Thai Rider Association said riders "ที่ไม่มีพื้นที่วิ่งประจำได้รับผลกระทบมากที่สุด" — [Prachatai 2022/11/101266](https://prachatai.com/journal/2022/11/101266); [Thai PBS 321133](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/321133); [The Thaiger 699531](https://thethaiger.com/th/news/699531/); riders also blocked Phetchaburi Rd over "ระบบรายได้การรับงานไม่เป็นธรรม" — [Prachachat news-1106506](https://www.prachachat.net/hilight-prachachat/news-1106506). Slots were described as ~2–3 h shifts with limited quota: "ไรเดอร์ที่จองรอบงานไม่ทันก็ไม่ได้ทำงานเลย" — [Prachatai 101266](https://prachatai.com/journal/2022/11/101266).
- **Oversupply upcountry [CONSENSUS].** Mahasarakham riders: base fare 17–19 THB vs 28 in Khon Kaen and "งานส่วนใหญ่เป็นงานระยะทางไกล"; the same coverage carried the complaint that long-serving riders get fewer jobs than newly registered ones — [Prachatai 2022/05/98567](https://prachatai.com/journal/2022/05/98567). LINE MAN Samut Songkhram riders said direct-assignment cuts income "ในพื้นที่ที่มีไรเดอร์มากกว่างาน" — [Prachatai 2022/05/98648](https://prachatai.com/journal/2022/05/98648). A Jan-2026 Indeed review (Malaysia) says orders are decreasing while riders are increasing — [Indeed Grab rider reviews](https://malaysia.indeed.com/cmp/Grab/reviews?fcountry=ALL&fjobtitle=Rider). Grab Thailand launched a 2026 part-time "side hustle" recruitment drive aimed at uniformed personnel and students, i.e., supply is still being added — [Telecomlover 2026-09-17](https://www.telecomlover.com/2026/09/17/grab-thailand-part-time-side-hustle-2026/).
- **Credit top-up belief [UNSUPPORTED].** A new rider: "ไปอ่านเจอมาเค้าแนะนำให้เติมเครดิต 1-2000บาท เพื่อจะให้มีงานเข้ามา" — no confirmation it works — [Pantip 41332519](https://pantip.com/topic/41332519).
- **Phone-related silence [ANECDOTE].** "ใช้ซัมซุง A20 งานไม่ค่อยเด้งเลย" — [Pantip 42705400](https://pantip.com/topic/42705400); rider equipment guides stress a phone with strong battery and accurate GPS plus a ≥20,000 mAh power bank — [Lemon8 equipment post](https://www.lemon8-app.com/@leefleefleef/7394547853210780161?region=us); [Priceza rider-phone guide](https://www.priceza.com/article/shopping-guide/smartphone/%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%B7%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%96%E0%B8%B7%E0%B8%AD-food-delivery.html).
- **Official levers that exist [OFFICIAL].** Auto Accept: "รับรายการอัตโนมัติ (Auto Accept) คือ ทางเลือกที่ช่วยให้คนขับรับรายการที่เข้ามาโดยอัตโนมัติ แทนที่คนขับจะต้องกดรับด้วยตนเองภายใน 10 วินาที" and "คนขับที่เปิดใช้งาน 'รับรายการอัตโนมัติ' จะยังมีโอกาสได้รับก่อน" — [GrabDriverTH auto-accept](https://www.grabdriverth.com/auto-accept); [new auto-accept method](https://www.grabdriverth.com/newfeatures/auto-accepted-24). Booking slots ("จองรอบขับ") exist for delivery — [GrabDriverTH delivery-home](https://www.grabdriverth.com/delivery-home); Bangkok 2W shift messaging: "รับใกล้ จบไว รายได้ดี" — [GrabDriverTH shift-bkk](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2022/shift-bkk). Korat has a "รอบจองลำดับพิเศษ! ออเดอร์ส่งหาคุณก่อนใคร" programme for GrabFood/GrabMart (details in KQ3) — [GrabDriverTH lcs-nakhon-ratchasima](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2025/bike/lcs-nakhon-ratchasima).
- **Grab's public position [OFFICIAL].** Grab Thailand says 99% of riders earn above minimum wage per hour — [MGR Online 9680000052164](https://mgronline.com/cyberbiz/detail/9680000052164); delivery earnings ~3x minimum wage and batching is limited to "ไม่เกิน 2 งาน" — [Thairath 2816229](https://www.thairath.co.th/money/tech_innovation/tech_companies/2816229). Platforms describe allocation as AI/algorithmic — [Sarakadee Lite](https://www.sarakadeelite.com/better-living/ai-fooddelivery-rider/).
- **Dormant accounts [OFFICIAL, 4W].** Accounts suspended for not taking jobs for over 6 months can self-reboard — [GrabDriverTH automate-reboard](https://www.grabdriverth.com/newfeatures/automate-reboard).

### Inferences
- The strongest structural explanation for "งานเงียบ" in a provincial city is supply/demand plus slot/zone prioritisation, not a hidden personal penalty. Riders without booked slots in the right zone are, by Grab's own programme language ("ออเดอร์ส่งหาคุณก่อนใคร"), behind slot-holders in the queue.
- "Fixes" that riders report (reinstall, clear cache, credit top-up, move spot) are indistinguishable from regression to the mean; none has evidence beyond single anecdotes.
- For the Korat user, the actionable, evidence-backed moves are: keep a consistent zone and book the priority slots (if offered in-app), turn on Auto Accept, and get AR/CR inside incentive gates (KQ4) — because Grab ties priority/eligibility to those metrics, not because the algorithm "remembers" individuals.

### Gaps
- No Pantip comment threads could be read in full; the comment-level "fixes that worked" remain unverified.
- No Facebook group (e.g., "ไรเดอร์ Grab", Korat rider groups), Blockdit or Dek-D content was reachable.
- No Grab Thailand statement explaining the allocation algorithm itself (distance, rating, AR weighting) was found.

## Key Question 2 — Do Thai riders report that declining or ignoring jobs reduces later job flow? Is there a consistent pattern (cooldown, fewer batches)?

### Takeaway
Yes: riders consistently report that declining lowers AR and that low AR leads to short lockouts and, eventually, warnings; Grab's own regional "Driver Timeout" feature confirms an immediate cooldown after ignored/declined jobs. There is no evidence of a long-term, individualised "shadow" reduction in job flow or of fewer batches after rejecting — that part is folklore.

### Cited Findings
- **Official Thai 2W standard [OFFICIAL].** From 19 พ.ค. 68 (2025), all service areas, motorcycle: "อัตราการรับ มากกว่า 50% และอัตราการยกเลิก ไม่เกิน 10%"; computed every Monday from the prior 7 days (e.g., Monday 26 พ.ค. 68 uses 19–25 พ.ค. 68); counting restarted from zero at launch; "ครั้งที่ 1-2 ตักเตือน ผ่านทางกล่องข้อความ" (later steps not surfaced) — [GrabDriverTH 2w-cr](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grabacademy/2w-cr). Definitions: acceptance rate = jobs accepted ÷ all jobs shown on the driver app; cancellation rate = jobs cancelled ÷ jobs accepted — [GrabDriverTH arcr](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grabacademy/arcr). 4W standard (older, 26 เม.ย. 2564): AR ≥ 80%, CR ≤ 10%, breach → in-app warning next day, standard exists "เพื่อป้องกันการระงับบัญชีผู้ขับ" — [GrabDriverTH 4w-ar-cr](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/4w-ar-cr).
- **Ignoring = declining for AR purposes [OFFICIAL by definition].** Because AR is accepted ÷ shown, letting a job time out ("ปล่อยให้งานหลุด") counts the same as pressing decline — [GrabDriverTH arcr](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grabacademy/arcr). The 2026 manual-accept screen auto-accepts if the driver takes no action within the set time, and a temporary "unable to accept" status can be applied for a set period depending on vehicle type — [GrabDriverTH default-accept 2026](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2026/car/default-accept).
- **Immediate cooldown is real (regional Grab policy) [OFFICIAL].** Grab's Driver Timeout: ignoring a job (~45 s) or closing an order on receipt brings up a temporary lock screen that stops new allocations until the driver taps Unlock; repeated ignores/cancels set the driver to "Unavailable" for a timed period, and the timeout lengthens with consecutive ignores (at least 30 s) — [Grab SG Driver Timeout](https://www.grab.com/sg/driver-timeout-feature-safeguarding-responsible-delivery-partners/); [Grab MY Driver Timeout](https://www.grab.com/my/blog/driver/car/driver-timeout-feature-protecting-high-performing-driver-partners/). No Thai-language page describing this lock was found, but Thai rider accounts match it (next bullet).
- **Thai rider-reported mechanics [ANECDOTE, consistent with official].** A Chiang Mai driver's explainer: manual accept gives ~15 s; frequent decliners sit at 20–30% AR vs 70–90% for normal riders "ซึ่งอาจมีผลต่อการได้รับงานในรอบถัดไป"; the system forces acceptance of at least 1 in every 10 jobs or imposes a 15- or 30-minute temporary suspension; cancellation ≤20% no effect, 20–35% penalty, >35% heavier, reset every Monday (these thresholds predate the 2025 standard) — surfaced from [Pantip 40179323 ชี้แจงจากคนขับ GRAB](https://pantip.com/topic/40179323) / [Pantip 42350347](https://pantip.com/topic/42350347).
- **Out-of-zone jobs force a dilemma [CONSENSUS].** "ระบบจ่ายงานให้วิ่งงานนอกโซนที่ไรเดอร์เลือกไว้บังคับต้องไป เพราะพอปฏิเสธเปอร์เซ็นต์การกดรับงานก็ลด !?" — declining out-of-zone jobs lowers AR and "ถ้าปฏิเสธเยอะก็อาจถูกพักงาน" — [Pantip 41885091](https://pantip.com/topic/41885091). Jobs nobody wants are typically "ปลายทางนอกเมืองหรือจุดที่ต้องตีรถเปล่ากลับ" — [Pantip 42421283 result set](https://pantip.com/topic/42421283).
- **Priority slots are explicitly gated on acceptance history [OFFICIAL].** In the Korat priority-slot programme, not accepting per the slot conditions counts as "ขาดการจอง 1 ครั้ง"; "หากขาดการจองครบ 3 ครั้ง ภายใน 4 วันติดต่อกัน จะถูกยกเลิกรอบอื่นๆ ที่จองไว้ พร้อมไม่ได้สิทธิ์จองรอบเป็นเวลา 2 วัน"; eligibility for a slot on 25 ส.ค. 66 was judged on acceptance history of 23–24 ส.ค. 66 — [GrabDriverTH lcs-nakhon-ratchasima](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2025/bike/lcs-nakhon-ratchasima). The Tak 2568 version requires "อัตราการรับ มากกว่า 70% และอัตราการยกเลิก น้อยกว่า 8%" — [GrabDriverTH lcs-tak](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2025/bike/lcs-tak); Phitsanulok uses 2 misses in 2 days — [GrabDriverTH lcs-phitsanulok](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2025/bike/lcs-phitsanulok).
- **Incentive gates use 90% / 5% [OFFICIAL].** Promo pages state "อัตราการรับตลอดระยะเวลาวันที่กำหนด มากกว่า 90%" and "อัตราการยกเลิกตลอดระยะเวลาวันที่กำหนด น้อยกว่า 5%" as incentive conditions, distinct from the quality standard — surfaced alongside [GrabDriverTH santagrab-bangkok 2025](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2025/bike/santagrab-bangkok) and [GrabDriverTH songkrangrab-bangkok 2026](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2026/bike/songkrangrab-bangkok). "Grab a Chance" eligibility also requires no complaints/suspensions and meeting service, acceptance and cancellation thresholds — [GrabDriverTH grabachance](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grabachance).
- **Singapore precedent [OFFICIAL/press].** Grab SG required ≥15% weekly acceptance with suspensions of 24 h to 2 weeks (older report) — [Firstlane SG](https://www.firstlane.com.sg/grab-suspends-drivers-low-acceptance-rating/). Grab's FAQ: transport cancellation rating is separate from food/mart/express job types — [Grab SG GSBonus FAQ](https://www.grab.com/sg/driver/transport/gsbonus/).
- **Batch/"งานซ้อน" and rejection [ANECDOTE].** Riders can switch off double jobs with a toggle ("ไม่อยากรับงานซ้อนแค่กดปุ่ม🟢นี้") — [TikTok @riderdekdoi](https://www.tiktok.com/@riderdekdoi/video/7376785045039615239); forum users say the toggle sometimes "ปิดไม่ทัน" and doesn't separate food types (drinks melt) — [Soccersuck 2008435](https://www.soccersuck.com/boards/topic/2008435/1). No source claims that rejecting single jobs reduces later batch offers.

### Inferences
- Pattern that IS supported: decline/ignore → (a) immediate lock/cooldown of seconds-to-minutes, escalating with consecutive ignores; (b) weekly AR falls; (c) below programme thresholds you lose incentives, priority slots and "Grab a Chance" eligibility; (d) below 50% you get warnings. That is a step-function on eligibility, not a continuous "fewer jobs forever" penalty.
- Pattern NOT supported: a persistent personal throttle ("โดนลดงานเพราะปฏิเสธ") beyond the above, or fewer batches after rejecting. Riders' perception of it is plausibly the cooldown plus loss of slot priority.
- For the user (AR 81.8% in some weeks): comfortably above the 50% standard but below the 90% incentive gate and the ~70% priority-slot style gate — the cost of declining is mostly lost top-ups, not account safety.

### Gaps
- Steps 3+ of the 2W enforcement ladder (after two warnings) were not surfaced.
- No Thai official or rider source quantifies the Thai timeout durations; the 15/30-min and "1 in 10" figures are one rider's account.
- Whether Korat's priority-slot thresholds equal Tak's 70%/8% is unknown.

## Key Question 3 — What do riders say about the best waiting spots (จุดรองาน) and why? Any Korat-specific advice?

### Takeaway
Thai sources reachable here contain surprisingly little concrete "where to wait" advice; what exists is (a) Grab's own framing that booked slots in a zone get "รับใกล้ จบไว" and priority, (b) rider complaints that proximity to a merchant does not by itself win the job, and (c) etiquette about not waiting inside restaurants. No Korat-specific spot advice (The Mall/Terminal 21/Central/หัวทะเล) was found; Korat-specific official material covers fare structure, incentives and priority slots only.

### Cited Findings
- **Proximity alone doesn't win jobs [CONSENSUS].** Riders near or "แทบจะติดร้านค้า" report not getting the order while a companion does — [Pantip 42421283](https://pantip.com/topic/42421283); [Pantip 41575171](https://pantip.com/topic/41575171).
- **Zone/slot framing [OFFICIAL].** Bangkok 2W slot messaging: "จองรอบขับ รับใกล้ จบไว รายได้ดี" — [GrabDriverTH shift-bkk](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2022/shift-bkk); Korat priority slots: "ออเดอร์ส่งหาคุณก่อนใคร จบไว สะสมรอบได้มากขึ้น" for GrabFood/GrabMart in "พื้นที่บริการ นครราชสีมา" — [GrabDriverTH lcs-nakhon-ratchasima](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2025/bike/lcs-nakhon-ratchasima). Riders in 2022 complained the company "บังคับโซน" and forced paired orders — [The Thaiger 699531](https://thethaiger.com/th/news/699531/).
- **Near vs far jobs [ANECDOTE].** Forum debate "ส่งใกล้ หรือส่งไกล คุ้มค่ากว่ากัน": near jobs often hit slow kitchens, wasting time — [Soccersuck 2139960](https://www.soccersuck.com/boards/topic/2139960/1). Upcountry, "งานส่วนใหญ่เป็นงานระยะทางไกล" raises fuel cost — [Prachatai 98567](https://prachatai.com/journal/2022/05/98567).
- **Restaurant etiquette [ANECDOTE, viral].** A rider was asked to wait outside a restaurant because he was not a customer; he complied and posted to warn others not to sit inside — [Khaosod 9689894](https://www.khaosod.co.th/special-stories/news_9689894); [LINE Today](https://today.line.me/th/v3/article/WBwQmqL).
- **Office pickups [OFFICIAL, Bangkok only].** Grab food lockers at The ParQ, FYI Center, The 9th etc. let riders avoid waiting for customers — [Thairath 2816229](https://www.thairath.co.th/money/tech_innovation/tech_companies/2816229).
- **Korat-specific official material [OFFICIAL].** New GrabFood/GrabMart fare structure in Nakhon Ratchasima from 9:00 on 15 พ.ค. 67 with wait time folded into the fare, a minimum fare per order and commission included — [GrabDriverTH ebp/nakhon-ratchasima](https://www.grabdriverth.com/ebp/nakhon-ratchasima); separate structure pages for Nang Rong — [GrabDriverTH ebp/nang-rong](https://www.grabdriverth.com/ebp/nang-rong). Korat 2W incentive page: "ขับทุกวัน รับอินเพชร สูงสุด 445 บ./วัน พร้อมรับรายได้รวมสูงสุด 525 บ./วัน ตั้งแต่วันนี้ – 30 พ.ย. 68" — [GrabDriverTH nakincentive](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2024/bike/nakincentive); Korat new-driver promo valid 1–31 ต.ค. 68 — [GrabDriverTH sign-up/nak](https://www.grabdriverth.com/sign-up/nak).
- **Korat retail geography (background only).** Terminal 21 Korat (700+ shops, Mittraphap Rd) — [Wongnai listing](https://www.wongnai.com/listings/restaurants-at-terminal-21-korat); three-mall competition Central/The Mall/Terminal 21 — [Positioning](https://positioningmag.com/strategic-move/60143). No rider source links these to job flow.
- **Weather as a demand driver [CONSENSUS, mixed].** "ในวันที่ฝนตก รถติด น้ำท่วม งานก็เด้งมาแบบไม่หยุด" — [TikTok แกร็บส่งของน้ำท่วม](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/%E0%B9%81%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%A3%E0%B9%87%E0%B8%9A%E0%B8%AA%E0%B9%88%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%82%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%99%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%B3%E0%B8%97%E0%B9%88%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%A1); customers complain riders are scarce in rain — [Pantip 43891037](https://pantip.com/topic/43891037); a rider explains deliberately not accepting in rain to keep service standards — [Lemon8 @khuntanriderv1](https://www.lemon8-app.com/@khuntanriderv1/7502969807145255432?region=th); a Chiang Mai Saturday diary (9:00–16:30) includes rain tips and slot booking via the "จองรอบ/ตั้งเวลาทำงาน" menu — [Lemon8 @.toony5](https://www.lemon8-app.com/@.toony5/7424160395889230337?region=us).
- **Time-of-day [CONSENSUS].** Peak recommendations 11:00–13:00 and 17:00–20:00 — [Lemon8 @crayonyellow5](https://www.lemon8-app.com/@crayonyellow5/7405565777790648849?region=us); an 18:00–23:00 earnings review (2568) — [Lemon8 @ohho.rider](https://www.lemon8-app.com/@ohho.rider/7511369260637291026?region=th). Grab's 2W shift-guarantee slots are 10:30–13:30, 13:30–16:30, 16:30–20:30 plus 9:00–10:30 or 20:30–22:00, i.e., Grab's own definition of the valuable hours — [GrabDriverTH shift-meg](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2025/bike/shift-meg).
- **Payday / holidays:** no Thai source found that evidences a "วันเงินเดือนออก" or holiday order spike for GrabFood (see Gaps).

### Inferences
- In Grab's current Thai design, "where to wait" matters less than "whether you hold a booked slot in the zone where you wait." Riders who sit near popular merchants without slots are the ones writing the "ติดร้านแต่ไม่ได้งาน" threads.
- For Korat (Hua Thale is east of the old city, near Terminal 21/The Mall corridor along Mittraphap), the only evidence-backed positioning advice is: stay inside the zone you booked, during the slot windows Grab itself guarantees (10:30–13:30, 16:30–20:30), and avoid dead-head returns from out-of-town drops because those are the jobs everyone else declines.

### Gaps
- No Thai rider content on "right side of the road", "away from rider crowds", or specific Korat spots was reachable; Facebook Korat rider groups were not accessible.
- Payday/holiday effects: no data found either way.

## Key Question 4 — What tactics do riders use to keep cancellation <5% and acceptance >90%, especially when a merchant is closed or food is very late?

### Takeaway
The official, zero-risk path exists: when the merchant is closed, riders may self-cancel with the correct reason and it does not count; merchants can cancel with "ร้านปิดแล้ว"; customer no-shows after the waiting period are compensated and excluded. The community folklore of "ให้ลูกค้ากดยกเลิกแทน" is real but is publicly condemned and, in the collusion form, listed by Grab as fraud. Long kitchen waits are now (by design) paid inside the fare rather than being a cancellation case.

### Cited Findings
- **Merchant closed → rider self-cancel is free [OFFICIAL].** "ร้านปิดให้บริการ" is a recognised necessary reason; "แกร็บขอแนะนำให้พาร์ทเนอร์ยกเลิกงานในกรณีที่จำเป็นเท่านั้น"; if the shop is closed the rider can cancel "โดยไม่ต้องขอให้ลูกค้า หรือติดต่อ Call Center ยกเลิกให้ ซึ่งไม่ส่งผลต่ออัตรายกเลิก" — [GrabDriverTH gf-modified-cr](https://www.grabdriverth.com/gf-bkk/gf-modified-cr). Out-of-stock/closed procedure for GrabFood with compensation: "การขอค่าชดเชยในกรณีสินค้าหมดหรือร้านค้าปิด" (do not just press cancel; follow the in-app flow) — [GrabDriverTH removing-oos](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2022/removing-oos). In-app help centre for fast reporting — [GrabDriverTH help-centre-hotline](https://www.grabdriverth.com/newfeatures/help-centre-hotline).
- **Customer no-show [OFFICIAL].** Contact the customer and wait ≥10 min (7 min for Saver types); cancelling with reason "หาผู้โดยสารไม่พบ" pays compensation (e.g., 10 THB/job GrabBike Bangkok) and "ไม่ส่งผลต่อเปอร์เซ็นต์การยกเลิกรายการ" — but choosing the wrong reason does count — [GrabDriverTH pax-no-show](https://www.grabdriverth.com/newfeatures/pax-no-show); [GrabDriverTH pax-cancellation](https://www.grabdriverth.com/pax-cancellation). Rider TikTok advice for unreachable customers: "ติดต่อ CC เพื่อขอยกเลิก" — [TikTok @riderfood56](https://www.tiktok.com/@riderfood56/video/7609943873107807508).
- **Merchant-side cancel rules [OFFICIAL].** Merchants cancel with reason "ร้านปิดแล้ว" only while the order is "ออเดอร์ใหม่" or "กำลังเตรียม"; once "ทำเสร็จแล้ว" the merchant cannot cancel and the rider or customer must; if nobody cancels the system auto-cancels after 1 hour; merchants are told "ไม่แนะนําให้ร้านค้ากดยกเลิกออเดอร์บ่อยครั้งในแต่ละวัน" — [Grab Merchant guide](https://merchant.grab.com/th-th/guides/default/edit-and-cancel-order); [Silom POS GrabFood cancel](https://support.silompos.com/th/article/grabfood-15pkmim/); merchants claim food cost within 7 days when no rider collects — [Grab Merchant claim](https://merchant.grab.com/th-th/blog/claim).
- **Long waits are paid, not a cancel case [OFFICIAL].** New GrabFood fare structure: "คิดค่ารออาหารรวมในค่ารอบ ไม่ต้องเคลมค่ารอทีหลัง" and "ยิ่งระยะเวลารออาหาร/สินค้านาน และส่งระยะทางไกล..." the fare rises — [GrabDriverTH ebp/bangkok](https://www.grabdriverth.com/ebp/bangkok); same design in Korat since 15 พ.ค. 67 — [GrabDriverTH ebp/nakhon-ratchasima](https://www.grabdriverth.com/ebp/nakhon-ratchasima). Counter-point from riders: platforms compute the wait fee from historical merchant data, not the actual wait — [Rocket Media Lab via Prachatai 112788](https://prachatai.com/journal/2025/05/112788).
- **"ให้ลูกค้ากดยกเลิกแทน" [CONSENSUS as practice, condemned].** 2025 news: a driver accepted then "เบี้ยว" claiming the price was unsatisfactory and "โยนภาระให้ลูกค้ายกเลิกหวังไม่เสียคะแนน" — [MGR Online 9680000030887](https://mgronline.com/onlinesection/detail/9680000030887); a 2020 driver-side explainer of why drivers ask customers to cancel — [Pantip 40179323](https://pantip.com/topic/40179323); a YouTube how-to titled "การยกเลิกงาน Grab แบบไม่เสียเปอร์เซ็นต์" exists (content not verified) — [YouTube sDHAHAVViHU](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sDHAHAVViHU). Grab lists colluding with a customer "โดยให้ผู้โดยสารกดยกเลิกงานทันทีเมื่อเรียกได้รถคันอื่น" as fraud — [GrabDriverTH fraud-activity](https://www.grabdriverth.com/fraud-activity) **[TOS]**.
- **Raise AR by not seeing bad jobs rather than declining them [OFFICIAL feature].** Turning off batch ("ปิดรับงานซ้อน") via the in-app toggle avoids declining double jobs — [TikTok @riderdekdoi](https://www.tiktok.com/@riderdekdoi/video/7376785045039615239); Auto Accept removes the manual-decline moment entirely — [GrabDriverTH auto-accept](https://www.grabdriverth.com/auto-accept).
- **Reference thresholds [OFFICIAL].** Quality standard 2W: AR >50%, CR ≤10%; incentive gates typically AR >90%, CR <5%; some priority-slot programmes AR >70%, CR <8% — [GrabDriverTH 2w-cr](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grabacademy/2w-cr); [GrabDriverTH santagrab-bangkok](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2025/bike/santagrab-bangkok); [GrabDriverTH lcs-tak](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2025/bike/lcs-tak). LINE MAN publishes its own cancellation-rate rules and a changed decline button — [LINE MAN cancellation rate](https://linemanpartner.com/rider/cancellation-rate/); [LINE MAN decline button](https://linemanpartner.com/blog-riderjourney-250723/).

### Inferences
- With CR at 6.3–8.8%, the user's gap to <5% is 1–2 cancels per week. The legitimate levers are: (1) never press plain cancel on closed/out-of-stock merchants — use the ร้านปิด/สินค้าหมด flow so it is excluded and compensated; (2) for unreachable customers, wait the full window and use the no-show reason; (3) switch off batch if batch orders are the ones being cancelled; (4) decline before accepting rather than cancel after (a decline costs AR, which is cheaper than CR at these thresholds, but preferably use Auto Accept plus a tighter zone so fewer bad offers arrive).
- Asking customers/merchants to cancel "แทน" is an account risk with no upside once the official exclusions are used.

### Gaps
- Whether a rider-initiated cancel for "อาหารช้ามาก" (merchant open, not out of stock) is excluded was not found; the structure suggests it is not.
- Exact compensation amounts for ร้านปิด cases in Korat were not surfaced.

## Key Question 5 — What app/phone/GPS practices are recommended, and which get accounts banned (mock location, modded apps, "โปรแกรมดักงาน")?

### Takeaway
Recommended practices are mundane (good-GPS phone, big battery/power bank, keep the official app updated, use Auto Accept). Grab Thailand explicitly classifies GPS/device-ID spoofing and modified Driver apps as Fair Play violations, runs an automated central fraud engine that can permanently suspend accounts, and Thai police have arrested makers/users of job-intercepting apps — hundreds of riders were implicated.

### Cited Findings
- **Phone/equipment [CONSENSUS].** Choose a phone with fast, accurate GPS even "ในซอกตึกหรือซอยแคบ", long battery, bright screen; carry a ≥20,000 mAh power bank — [Priceza rider phones](https://www.priceza.com/article/shopping-guide/smartphone/%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%B7%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%96%E0%B8%B7%E0%B8%AD-food-delivery.html); [SpecPhone](https://specphone.com/web/6-high-perf-smartphone-for-grab/288750); [VN Phone 2026](https://www.vnphonemobile.com/blog/mobile-phone-for-riders/); [Pantip 42705400](https://pantip.com/topic/42705400); [Pantip 40695217](https://pantip.com/topic/40695217). "Turn off high-accuracy GPS to save battery" contradicts every guide found (no source recommends it).
- **Restart/cache/reinstall [UNSUPPORTED as a job-flow tactic].** Only support's stock advice to reinstall/log in again — [Pantip 39976139](https://pantip.com/topic/39976139) — and a rider who closed the app for two days and then got nothing — [Pantip 41143613](https://pantip.com/topic/41143613). LINE MAN publishes an app-freeze fix — [LINE MAN app freeze](https://linemanwongnai.my.salesforce-sites.com/rd/rdarticles?id=4407184449561-kaaraekaikhpayhaaae). No source shows restart/cache-clearing increases jobs.
- **Auto Accept priority [OFFICIAL].** "คนขับที่เปิดใช้งาน 'รับรายการอัตโนมัติ' จะยังมีโอกาสได้รับก่อน" — [GrabDriverTH auto-accept](https://www.grabdriverth.com/auto-accept); back-to-back jobs are auto-added while on — [Grab MY Auto Accept](https://www.grab.com/my/blog/driver/car/auto-accept-for-an-easier-drive-with-grab/).
- **Fraud engine and permanent suspension [OFFICIAL].** "เพื่อป้องกันความเสียหายที่อาจเกิดขึ้นจากการแฝงตัวของมิจฉาชีพ Grab ได้นำระบบอัจฉริยะที่ใช้นโยบายประมวลผล และสั่งการจากทีมพัฒนาและสนับสนุนทางเทคนิคส่วนกลาง เข้ามาใช้ในประเทศไทย"; flagged patterns include jobs whose time/frequency/distance are unreasonable, especially "กดส่งงานก่อนถึงจุดหมายจน GPS ไม่มีการเคลื่อนไหว"; some behaviours lead to permanent app suspension — [GrabDriverTH fraud-activity](https://www.grabdriverth.com/fraud-activity).
- **Fair Play tiers [OFFICIAL] [TOS].** Low tier: "ปลอมแปลงอุปกรณ์ เช่น GPS, ID ของอุปกรณ์"; high tier: "การใช้แอปพลิเคชัน Grab Driver ที่ได้รับการแก้ไขหรือไม่ถูกต้องซึ่งไม่ได้มาจาก Google Play Store หรือ Apple App Store" — [GrabDriverTH fair-play](https://www.grabdriverth.com/fair-play). Security guide: "Using an impersonated GPS application on your device is prohibited" and unofficial driver apps are detected and blocked — [Grab TH security guide](https://www.grab.com/th/en/security/security-guide-dax/). Code of conduct violations include "Deliberately using faulty device/handphone which impairs GPS" and "False completion of trips – completing ride or order without arriving at actual pickup or dropoff point" — [Grab TH driver code of conduct](https://www.grab.com/th/en/terms-policies/code-of-conduct-driver/); regulations page last updated 11 ส.ค. 2569 — [GrabDriverTH grab-regulation](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grab-regulation).
- **"โปรแกรมดักงาน" — rider outrage and police cases [CONSENSUS + ILLEGAL].** TikTok: "ระบาดหนัก โปรแกรมช่วยรับงานไรเดอร์ Lineman Grab คนดีๆต้องรับงานไกลไปหมด" — [TikTok @1nakub](https://www.tiktok.com/@1nakub/video/7629986750596648200). Police arrested a man who hired a programmer to build an app that "ดักจับงานจากระบบของไรเดอร์ไปให้กับคนที่ใช้โดยอัตโนมัติ", locking restaurants/orders/pickup points; Robinhood reported damage since 2565 — [Thairath crime 2775861](https://www.thairath.co.th/news/crime/2775861); cyber police case with ~500 riders implicated for using an order-interception system — [Bangkokbiznews 1120599](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/news/news-update/1120599).
- **Mock-GPS tutorials exist but are foreign and against policy [TOS].** YouTube "grab rider fake gps / hide mock location" shorts (Indian) — [YouTube VVctwQsq4Rs](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/VVctwQsq4Rs); fake-GPS apps on Play Store — [Play Store fake GPS](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=top.tinysoft.fakegps&hl=en_US). These are documented here only as the thing Grab's Fair Play rules target.
- **Two phones / multi-apping [ANECDOTE, legal status unclear].** Riders are informal workers with no direct employer ("ไรเดอร์ (เช่น Grab, LINE MAN, Shopee ฯลฯ) ไม่มีนายจ้างโดยตรง") and multi-app discussion is routine — [Pantip 43298261](https://pantip.com/topic/43298261/desktop); a merchant-side review notes LINE MAN's system forces double jobs, slowing pickups — [Lemon8 merchant review](https://www.lemon8-app.com/@sandwich_coffee.factory/7384451612774908433?region=us). No Grab TH rule against multi-apping was found; the risk is indirect (late pickups → cancellations/ratings; a second device must not be used for GPS/ID spoofing).

### Inferences
- The only phone-side tactic with official backing is Auto Accept; everything else is hygiene (GPS accuracy, battery, official app build).
- The user's current account risk from legitimate behaviour is nil; the real bans are reserved for spoofing, modded APKs, interception bots and fake completions — all of which the fraud engine targets and some of which (bots) carry criminal exposure in Thailand.

### Gaps
- No Grab TH statement specifically on multi-apping or on running two devices was found.
- Thai-language ban testimonials for mock GPS were not surfaced (search budget exhausted).

## Key Question 6 — Is there community knowledge about the "การปรับยอด" top-up: when it is higher or lower, and does it change with distance, time or batch?

### Takeaway
No Thai rider-community discussion of "การปรับยอด" as such was found. Official material shows three mechanisms that would appear as an adjustment/top-up line: a guaranteed minimum fare per order under the 2023–24 GrabFood structure, shift-based daily earnings guarantees ("รายได้ต่อวันไม่ถึง แกร็บเติมให้"), and (4W) a distance/time shortfall top-up. The user's observation that ~half of earnings is top-up is consistent with low base fares in provinces.

### Cited Findings
- **Minimum fare per order [OFFICIAL].** "กำหนดค่ารอบขั้นต่ำในทุกออเดอร์ ไม่มีออเดอร์ที่รายได้น้อยกว่าขั้นต่ำที่กำหนด"; "รับค่ารอบเต็มๆ ไม่หักเพิ่ม ค่าคอมฯ รวมอยู่ในค่ารอบแล้ว"; wait time and distance raise the fare — [GrabDriverTH ebp/bangkok](https://www.grabdriverth.com/ebp/bangkok) (from 3 เม.ย. 2566); Korat version from 15 พ.ค. 67 with the same elements — [GrabDriverTH ebp/nakhon-ratchasima](https://www.grabdriverth.com/ebp/nakhon-ratchasima); Chonburi from 4 มิ.ย. 67 — [GrabDriverTH ebp/chonburi](https://www.grabdriverth.com/ebp/chonburi).
- **Shift guarantee [OFFICIAL].** "[รถจักรยานยนต์] จองรอบขับต่อเนื่อง รายได้ต่อวันไม่ถึง แกร็บเติมให้!" — option 2 pays up to ~1,100 THB/day for booking 10:30–13:30, 13:30–16:30, 16:30–20:30 plus 9:00–10:30 or 20:30–22:00 (threshold/formula not surfaced) — [GrabDriverTH shift-meg](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2025/bike/shift-meg). Korat incentive: diamonds up to 445 THB/day and total up to 525 THB/day to 30 พ.ย. 68 — [GrabDriverTH nakincentive](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2024/bike/nakincentive).
- **Shortfall top-up (4W, Bangkok 10 มี.ค. 2568) [OFFICIAL].** "แกร็บสมทบส่วนต่างปรับรายได้ให้เพิ่มขึ้น กรณีที่ผู้โดยสารจ่ายค่าโดยสารต่ำกว่าระยะทางและเวลาที่ผู้ให้บริการใช้จริงตั้งแต่จุดรับบุ๊คกิ้ง" — [GrabDriverTH eagle-bkk](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/eagle-bkk); provincial 4W version — [GrabDriverTH eagle-otc](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/2026/car/eagle-otc). New earnings page: "รายได้สุทธิ ค่ารอบซึ่งหักค่าคอมมิชชัน และผ่านการปรับรายได้เรียบร้อยแล้ว" with a "การปรับรายได้" line for items like loan repayments/app fees — [GrabDriverTH ttsp-th](https://www.grabdriverth.com/newfeatures/ttsp-th). GrabFood "เห็นรายได้ก่อนรับงาน" (two-sided pricing) page exists; details not surfaced — [GrabDriverTH 2side-pricing](https://www.grabdriverth.com/gf-bkk/2side-pricing).
- **Base fares are low and falling [CONSENSUS, news].** Fares fell from 62 THB (2564) to 30–43, some provinces 15 THB; batch orders not paid double and batched orders had reduced fares — [The Active Thai PBS 2024-03-05](https://theactive.thaipbs.or.th/news/socialmovement-20240305); GrabFood Bangkok flat 30 THB + wait fee after the strike — [Rocket Media Lab](https://rocketmedialab.co/rider-delivery-decline/); raw data to 30 เม.ย. 2568 — [Rocket Media Lab database](https://rocketmedialab.co/database-rider-delivery-decline/); Khon Kaen riders struck in Oct 2568 after per-round pay fell to ~21 THB — [Thai Post 881512](https://www.thaipost.net/district-news/881512/); Mahasarakham 17–19 THB (2022) — [Prachatai 98567](https://prachatai.com/journal/2022/05/98567); Al Jazeera: per-order 40→38 THB, daily 2,000→900 — [Al Jazeera 2023-09-08](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2023/9/8/thailands-food-delivery-drivers-see-wages-slump-as-platforms-cut-costs); Roojai 2567: base 28 THB Bangkok, business zones 43 — [Roojai](https://www.roojai.com/article/lifestyle/top-income-of-food-delivery-riders/).
- **Tiers and diamonds [OFFICIAL].** Partner tiers Member/Silver/Gold/Platinum/Hero are computed from the previous month's performance and give perks (loans, discounts), not job priority; diamond incentives ("เพชร") add diamonds for completing missions — [GrabDriverTH incentive](https://www.grabdriverth.com/grabacademy/incentive); [GrabDriverTH premium-benefits](https://www.grabdriverth.com/news/premium-benefits).

### Inferences
- "การปรับยอด" in the user's weekly report is most plausibly the sum of (i) minimum-fare top-ups on orders where the customer-paid delivery fee is below the guaranteed per-order minimum, (ii) incentive/diamond payouts and shift guarantees. If so it should be higher on short, cheap orders (bigger gap to the minimum), on batch legs with reduced fares, and in weeks where incentive gates (AR >90%, CR <5%) are met; it will drop sharply in weeks where those gates are missed — which matches the user's pattern of AR 81.8% / CR 6.3–8.8% weeks.
- A ~50% top-up share implies the Korat base fare is near the provincial floor (15–21 THB range reported elsewhere), so protecting incentive eligibility is worth more than chasing marginal extra orders.

### Gaps
- No rider source or official page defines "การปรับยอด" line-by-line; the shift-guarantee threshold and formula were not surfaced.
- No data on whether top-up varies with distance or batch beyond the structural inference above.

### Inferences
- The community's working model ("the system punishes decliners and remembers you") is a folk rendering of real but mechanical rules: timeout locks, weekly AR/CR gates, and slot eligibility.
- Everything that actually moves job flow for a compliant rider in Korat is in the official stack: slot + zone + Auto Accept + metric gates; the user's weekly stats place them just outside the gates, which is where the "half of income is top-up" leverage sits.
